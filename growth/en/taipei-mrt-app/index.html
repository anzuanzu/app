Taipei MRT App: Route Planner, Fares & Arrival Reminder

A practical checklist for visitors and commuters who need one place to compare routes, fares, train intervals and arrival reminders.

Quick answer

Start with your real origin and destination, compare transfers and fares, then check train intervals and service notices. Turn on an arrival reminder only after the route is confirmed.

What to check before you ride

✦Route planning should include transfers, station exits and walking time.

✦Fares, first/last trains and service status can change; verify them before departure.

✦A multilingual interface helps visitors, while saved routes reduce repeated searches for commuters.

Three steps for a smoother trip

  1. 01

    Enter the actual origin and destination, not just a landmark name.

  2. 02

    Compare transfers, fare, estimated time and the best station exit.

  3. 03

    Check official service notices, then use an arrival reminder if helpful.

Frequently asked questions

What should I check in a Taipei MRT app first?

Check route planning, transfers, fares, train intervals and first/last train information before choosing an arrival reminder workflow.

Can an app replace official Taipei Metro notices?

No. Use the app for trip planning and reminders, but verify disruptions, construction and service changes with official Taipei Metro information.
